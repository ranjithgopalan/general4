STAKEHOLDER INTERVIEW NOTES
Insurance Policy Management System
Interview Date: January 15, 2026
Interviewer: Jane Doe, Business Analyst

===========================================
INTERVIEW 1: Sarah Johnson - Senior Insurance Agent
===========================================

Q: What are the biggest pain points in your current workflow?
A: The biggest issue is the time it takes to create a new policy. I have to fill out paper forms, manually calculate premiums, and then enter everything into multiple systems. It takes about 45 minutes per policy, and I often make calculation errors that get caught later.

Q: How many policies do you typically create per day?
A: On a good day, I can complete 8-10 policies. But with all the manual work, I'm exhausted by the end of the day.

Q: What features would you most want in a new system?
A: Definitely automated premium calculation! I also want the ability to save drafts and come back to them later. And please make it work on my iPad - I do a lot of fieldwork and need mobile access.

Q: What information do you typically need to collect from customers?
A: Personal details like name, date of birth, address, contact information. For the policy itself: coverage amount, policy type, beneficiary information, and any pre-existing conditions for health policies.

Q: How do you currently handle policy approvals?
A: I email my manager with the policy details, and they review and email back approval. It's slow and there's no way to track where policies are in the approval process.

===========================================
INTERVIEW 2: Michael Chen - Regional Policy Manager
===========================================

Q: What are your main responsibilities regarding policy management?
A: I approve all policies over $500,000 and review any policies that have high risk factors. I also monitor our team's performance and ensure we're meeting compliance requirements.

Q: How do you currently track pending approvals?
A: I don't have a good system. Agents email me, and I keep a mental note of what needs review. I probably miss some, to be honest.

Q: What reporting capabilities would you need?
A: I need to see team performance metrics - how many policies created, average processing time, error rates. I also need compliance reports showing that we're following all the rules for age limits, coverage amounts, etc.

Q: What's your biggest concern about implementing a new system?
A: Agent adoption. Some of our older agents are very comfortable with paper forms and resist change. We'll need good training and ongoing support.

Q: What approval turnaround time would be acceptable?
A: Ideally same day. If I get a notification when a policy needs review, I can usually approve within 1-2 hours during business hours.

===========================================
INTERVIEW 3: Lisa Rodriguez - Compliance Officer
===========================================

Q: What compliance requirements must the system meet?
A: We have to comply with state insurance regulations, which vary by state. For health insurance, HIPAA is critical. We also need SOC 2 certification for our customers who require it.

Q: What data retention requirements exist?
A: Policy records must be kept for 7 years per regulatory requirements. We need full audit trails showing who created, modified, or accessed each policy.

Q: What security measures are most important?
A: Encryption of all PII - both at rest and in transit. Role-based access control so agents can only see their own policies. Multi-factor authentication for system access. And we need automatic session timeouts.

Q: How should the system handle policy changes?
A: Every change must be logged with the user ID, timestamp, what was changed, and why. The audit log must be immutable - no one can delete or modify it.

Q: What reporting do you need for compliance?
A: Monthly reports showing any policies that violate business rules, any data access by users, and any system security events. We also need ad-hoc reporting for regulatory audits.

===========================================
INTERVIEW 4: Tom Wilson - IT Support Lead
===========================================

Q: What technical constraints do we need to consider?
A: We're standardized on AWS for infrastructure. All new systems must integrate with our LDAP for authentication. We use Oracle for our main database, so integration there is important.

Q: What are your concerns about system performance?
A: End of month is our peak time - agents rush to meet quotas. We need the system to handle at least 500 concurrent users without degrading performance.

Q: What integrations are required?
A: We need to integrate with our CRM for customer data, the product catalog system for policy types and pricing rules, and our document management system (SharePoint) for storing policy documents.

Q: What's your preference for the user interface?
A: It needs to be responsive and work on tablets since agents use iPads in the field. It should follow our company design standards and be accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA).

Q: How should we handle system maintenance and updates?
A: We have a maintenance window on Saturday nights 11 PM to 2 AM. Updates should be deployable with zero downtime if possible. We need good monitoring and alerting.

===========================================
INTERVIEW 5: Karen Martinez - Customer Service Representative
===========================================

Q: How do customers currently interact with the policy system?
A: They don't - it's all through agents. But customers frequently call us asking about policy status, coverage details, and how to make changes.

Q: What would improve the customer experience?
A: If customers could view their policies online, that would reduce calls to us. Also, if the policy creation process was faster, customers wouldn't have to wait days for confirmation.

Q: What questions do customers most frequently ask?
A: "When will my policy be active?" "What's my coverage amount?" "How do I add a beneficiary?" "Can I change my coverage?" "How do I file a claim?"

Q: What customer-facing features would you recommend?
A: A customer portal where they can view policies, download documents, and request changes. Email notifications when policy status changes. Online chat for simple questions.

Q: How do you handle customer complaints about the current system?
A: Most complaints are about how long it takes to get a policy. Customers get frustrated waiting. If we can speed that up, satisfaction will improve dramatically.

===========================================
KEY TAKEAWAYS
===========================================

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Reduce policy creation time from 45 minutes to under 15 minutes
- Automated premium calculation to eliminate errors
- Mobile/tablet support for field agents
- Automated approval workflow with notifications
- Comprehensive compliance and audit logging
- Integration with existing systems (CRM, LDAP, Oracle, SharePoint)

MUST-HAVE FEATURES:
- Guided policy creation workflow
- Real-time validation of business rules
- Dashboard for managers showing pending approvals
- Performance and compliance reporting
- Role-based access control
- Multi-factor authentication

NICE-TO-HAVE FEATURES:
- Customer self-service portal
- Advanced search and filtering
- Bulk operations for multiple policies
- Mobile app (native iOS/Android)

SUCCESS METRICS:
- Policy creation time: 45 min → 15 min (70% reduction)
- Error rate: 12% → <1% (95% reduction)
- Customer satisfaction: 3.2 → 4.5 (41% improvement)
- System availability: 99.9% uptime
- Manager approval time: Same day (within 2 hours)

RISKS TO ADDRESS:
- Agent resistance to change (mitigation: training, super-users)
- Data migration complexity (mitigation: pilot migration, validation)
- Integration reliability (mitigation: fallback mechanisms, monitoring)
- Peak load performance (mitigation: load testing, auto-scaling)
