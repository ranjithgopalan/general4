# Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) Disclaimer

Standard disclaimer for all validation reports emphasizing the need for human verification.

## Overview

All AIDLC-validate findings require human verification before action. This disclaimer must be included in every validation report to set appropriate expectations about AI limitations.

---

## Standard Disclaimer for Reports

Include this section prominently in all validation reports:

```markdown
## IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER

This report is generated by AI and requires human verification.

| Limitation | Impact |
|------------|--------|
| **Semantic matching** | May miss paraphrased requirements or use different terminology |
| **Context limits** | Large documents may be partially processed |
| **Confidence levels** | Estimates based on heuristics, not guarantees |
| **False positives** | May report gaps that exist under different names |
| **False negatives** | May miss coverage due to terminology differences |

**Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) is MANDATORY before taking action on any finding.**

### Accuracy Estimates

| Validation Type | Estimated Accuracy | Human Verification Level |
|-----------------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| Document-to-Document (EPIC, Features, Stories) | ~70-80% | Moderate review needed |
| Code Traceability | ~60-70% | Thorough review needed |
| Semantic Matching | ~50-60% | Extensive review needed |

### Why Human Verification is Required

1. **Paraphrasing:** Requirements may be stated differently in target documents
2. **Implicit Coverage:** Some requirements may be covered implicitly
3. **Different Terminology:** Same concept expressed with different words
4. **Context Nuances:** AI may miss contextual relationships
5. **Intentional Gaps:** Some items may be deliberately deferred or out of scope
```

---

## Confidence-Specific Guidance

### For HIGH Confidence Findings

```markdown
**HIGH Confidence Finding**

While confidence is HIGH, human verification is still required because:
- Explicit ID match doesn't guarantee semantic alignment
- Requirements may have been moved or refactored
- Context may indicate intentional exclusion

**Action:** Brief verification (5-10 minutes) recommended before proceeding
```

### For MEDIUM Confidence Findings

```markdown
**MEDIUM Confidence Finding**

Careful review is essential because:
- Partial matches may be sufficient or insufficient depending on context
- Synonyms may or may not capture the full requirement
- Requirements may be split across multiple artifacts

**Action:** Careful review (15-30 minutes) required before proceeding
```

### For LOW Confidence Findings

```markdown
**LOW Confidence Finding**

Thorough investigation is MANDATORY because:
- Content-based matches have high false positive rate
- Fuzzy matching may identify unrelated content
- Requirements may use completely different terminology
- Gap may not exist at all

**Action:** Thorough investigation (30-60 minutes) required. Do NOT act without verification.
```

## Report-Level HITL Guidance

### Executive Summary Disclaimer

```markdown
## Coverage Estimates (Requires Verification)

**Overall Estimated Coverage:** ~X% (+/- 15% margin of error)

This percentage is an AI estimate based on content matching. Actual coverage may be higher or lower depending on:
- Paraphrasing in target documents
- Implicit vs explicit coverage
- Terminology differences
- Context not captured by AI

**Do not make project decisions based on this estimate alone.**
```

### Recommendations Section Disclaimer

```markdown
## Recommendations (Verify Before Acting)

The following recommendations are based on AI analysis and may not be appropriate:

### Before Acting on Any Recommendation:
1. Verify the gap actually exists
2. Check if coverage exists under different terminology
3. Confirm the requirement is still valid (not deferred/cancelled)
4. Assess impact and priority with stakeholders
5. Validate with subject matter experts

**Never auto-implement recommendations without verification.**
```

---

## Gap-Level HITL Guidance

Every gap entry must include:

```markdown
- **Human Action Required:** {specific verification instruction}
```

### Examples by Confidence Level

**HIGH Confidence Gap:**
```markdown
- **Human Action Required:** Verify this gap exists. Check if requirement was intentionally deferred. If real gap confirmed, prioritize for implementation.
```

**MEDIUM Confidence Gap:**
```markdown
- **Human Action Required:** Investigate carefully. May exist under different name or be covered implicitly. Verify with team before acting.
```

**LOW Confidence Gap:**
```markdown
- **Human Action Required:** Thorough investigation required. High probability of false positive. Do NOT act until verified by subject matter expert.
```

---

## Limitations to Acknowledge

### AI Validation Cannot:
- Understand business context and priorities
- Distinguish intentional gaps from oversights
- Interpret implicit coverage
- Assess quality or correctness (only coverage)
- Make project decisions

### Human Review Must:
- Validate all findings before action
- Assess business impact and priority
- Determine if gaps are intentional
- Verify coverage under different terminology
- Make final decisions on recommendations

## Usage in Validators

Validators should include this disclaimer in their reports:

```markdown
## Human Verification Required

See `skills/validation-strategies/hitl-disclaimer.md` for complete HITL guidance.

**Summary:**
- All findings require human verification
- Estimated accuracy: {X}%
- Confidence levels are estimates, not guarantees
- Do not act on LOW confidence findings without thorough review
```
